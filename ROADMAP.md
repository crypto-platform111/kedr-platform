# KEDR roadmap after 0.2

Version 0.2 connects blank/guided/visual creation, a broader OHLCV DSL, historical financial charts, real decision traces, bounded comparisons, real local ML examples and an optional consent-gated AI adapter. Existing research and version-1 runs remain protected. The items below are remaining work, not activated services.

## Next local product work

- Improve the guided builder beyond its bounded MA/RSI family while retaining the same typed graph and preview semantics.
- Extend saved chart preferences to full multi-window layouts/drawing tools. Current terminal supports one oscillator pane and bounded actual history windows.
- Improve large-graph performance/accessibility/localization catalogs and mobile editing; preserve the existing stable XYFlow update boundary.
- Add pagination and explicit storage/retention quotas for strategies, results and audit records. Preserve immutable provenance and owner checks.
- Keep engine/schema/data hashes stable; introduce new versions when accounting/formulas/source semantics change rather than reinterpret old results.

## Research/provider work

- Add source-specific adapters only after permission/provenance/version/quality/receipt audits. Provider contracts alone do not establish connectivity.
- A trained model needs an explicit compatible as-of prediction adapter, artifact validation, feature availability and horizon checks before any ML graph node can run. No automatic promotion.
- Derivatives carry requires synchronized Spot/perpetual executable quotes, settled funding, hedge/margin/borrow/accounting and costs. Indicative rates and candle spreads do not establish attainable carry.
- Microstructure/liquidation research needs genuine exchange labels/sequence/receipt data, outages and quality controls; never infer liquidation from a price jump.
- A separately authorized live recorder needs bounded storage, receipt capture, reconciliation and reservation clearance. No dates or collector changes are scheduled.
- External AI connectivity must be proven only with authorized configuration/consent, conservative pricing ceilings and observed request evidence. No paid call is needed to keep core workflows usable.
- Licensed/permitted news ingestion needs publication/receipt/version/deduplication/citation and sentiment provenance. Fixtures must stay labelled.

## Statistical integrity

Fixed scenarios are descriptive policy replays, not optimized strategies or adaptive model walk-forward training. Week-cluster intervals are approximate/unadjusted and suppressed for sparse coverage. Historical app experiments do not become independent validation through chronology.

Any prospective information test needs a separately frozen meaningful effect/precision plan, strong baselines, receipt-safe inputs and genuinely unreserved outcomes. No automatic launch follows an attractive chart or local ML score.

## Before access from other machines

Replace development/preview servers with TLS/reverse proxy/supervision; harden origin/host/CSP/cookies, identity recovery/MFA and abuse limits. Add durable workers/leases/retries, cross-process admission and hard process limits for general computation. ML currently has a supervised subprocess; backtest/scenario threads are not a distributed sandbox.

Test backup/restore, disk quotas/retention, operational monitoring/privacy/moderation, secret handling and incident response. Review data licenses/redistribution and deployment security. PostgreSQL remains a scaling option, not a requirement for the local release.

## Deliberately separate products

Paper/live order routing, private exchange accounts, portfolio/short/leverage execution, payments/marketplace, delivered notifications and automated strategy/model deployment remain disconnected. They require their own accounting/security/permission designs. Existing protected research, market databases and collector state remain outside those proposals.
